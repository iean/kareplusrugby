We are recruiting **waking-night care assistants for care homes** across
**Northampton and Leicestershire**. If nights alone do not give you the hours
you want, we can add **daytime home-care visits** around them, so you can build
a week that fits your life.

## Nights are a different job to days

On a night shift you are one of a small team responsible for the whole home
while people sleep. The work is settling residents at the start of the night,
doing regular comfort and safety rounds, repositioning people who need it to
protect their skin, and being a calm, reassuring presence for anyone who wakes
unsettled or confused. It is quieter than a day shift, but you carry more of the
responsibility yourself and you often decide when something needs escalating.
It suits people who are level-headed and comfortable working with less of a
crowd around them.

## Where you will work

This is our most spread-out area, so being matched to the right home matters
more here than anywhere. There is night work in and around **Northampton** and
out towards Daventry, and across **Leicestershire** from the city into the
market towns. Because these are overnight shifts and public transport is thin at
those hours, being able to drive makes a real difference to how much we can
offer you — tell us your situation and we will be honest about what is nearby.

## You do not need care experience

We train people who are new to care — a full induction, the Care Certificate,
and all the mandatory training the role needs. On nights, what counts most is
being calm, observant and completely dependable.

## What you get

- **National Living Wage plus 12.07% rolled-up holiday pay**, paid every
  payday — the exact rate is at the top of this page
- A named coordinator who knows you, and **someone on call all night**
- Support towards **NVQ Level 2 and 3** if you want to go further
- The full list of what we offer is on our [careers page](/careers)

## What we ask

- That you are reliable and turn up when you said you would — nights depend on it
- That you treat people with dignity, on their worst day as well as their best
- All roles are subject to an **enhanced DBS check** and satisfactory references
