This file exists to show the format. **It is not a real job advert** and it does
not appear on the site, because `draft: true` in the front matter hides it.

To advertise a real role: copy this file, give it a new name such as
`care-assistant-nights.md`, replace every field with real detail, and set
`draft: false`.

Everything below the front matter is the body of the advert, written in
markdown. Use it for the day-to-day of the role, what someone needs to bring,
and what they get in return.

Do not put a pay figure in the `pay` field unless it is real and agreed. If you
leave it out, the card tells the applicant that pay is discussed on
application, which is true and is better than a guess.
