We are recruiting **waking-night care assistants for care homes** across
**Rugby and Coventry**. If you want more hours than nights alone give you, we
can add **daytime home-care visits** around your shifts — so you can build the
week that suits you.

## Nights are a different job to days

A night shift is quieter, but it asks more of your judgement. You are one of a
small team looking after the whole home while people sleep. The work is settling
residents at the start of the shift, doing comfort and safety rounds through the
night, repositioning people who need it to protect their skin, and being calm
and reassuring for anyone who wakes confused or distressed. You will handle
whatever the night brings, and you will often be the person who decides when to
call for help. It suits people who are steady under pressure and happy working
with a bit more independence.

## Where you will work

Rugby is our home town — our office is on Central Park, so you always have a
real person to reach, not just a voice on a phone. Coventry, a short drive away,
is a city with more and larger homes and a steady demand for reliable night
staff. The patch is compact, so travel to and from a shift is short.

## You do not need care experience

We train people who are new to care — a full induction, the Care Certificate,
and all the mandatory training the role needs. What matters most on nights is
that you are calm, observant and dependable.

## What you get

- **National Living Wage plus 12.07% rolled-up holiday pay**, paid every
  payday — the exact rate is at the top of this page
- **Mileage paid** on any home-care visits you pick up to top up your hours
- A named coordinator who knows you, and **someone on call all night**
- Support towards **NVQ Level 2 and 3** if you want to go further
- The full list of what we offer is on our [careers page](/careers)

## What we ask

- That you are reliable and turn up when you said you would — nights depend on it
- That you treat people with dignity, on their worst day as well as their best
- All roles are subject to an **enhanced DBS check** and satisfactory references
