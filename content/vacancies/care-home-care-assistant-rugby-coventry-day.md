We are recruiting **care assistants for day shifts in care homes** across
**Rugby and Coventry**. If you want to build your hours up, we can add
**daytime home-care visits** around your care-home shifts — you tell us how
many hours you want and we fit the work around it.

## What a day shift is actually like

Days in a care home are busy and varied. You will be helping residents get up,
washed and dressed in the morning, supporting them through breakfast, lunch and
tea, helping with medication rounds where you are trained to, and being part of
the activities and the conversation that make a home feel like one. Families
visit during the day, so you are often the friendly face they see and the
person they ask how their mum or dad is doing. There are more of you on shift
during the day than at night, so it is a good place to learn the job well.

## Where you will work

Rugby is our home town — our office is on Central Park, so this is the one area
where you can walk in and talk to us in person if something needs sorting.
Coventry is a short drive away and has more and larger homes. It is a compact
patch, so you spend less of your day travelling and more of it doing the job.

## You do not need care experience

Plenty of our best carers came from retail, hospitality, or from caring for
their own family. If you are reliable, patient and genuinely interested in
people, we will train you in the rest — a full induction, the Care Certificate,
and all the mandatory training the role needs.

## What you get

- **National Living Wage plus 12.07% rolled-up holiday pay**, paid every
  payday — the exact rate is at the top of this page
- **Mileage paid** on any home-care visits you pick up to top up your hours
- A named coordinator who knows you, and **someone on call out of hours**
- Support towards **NVQ Level 2 and 3** if you want to go further
- The full list of what we offer is on our [careers page](/careers)

## What we ask

- That you are reliable and turn up when you said you would
- That you treat people with dignity, on their worst day as well as their best
- All roles are subject to an **enhanced DBS check** and satisfactory references
