We are recruiting **care assistants** across **Coventry, Rugby, Leicester and
Northampton**, for both sides of what we do: covering shifts in care homes, and
visiting people in their own homes.

## You do not need care experience

Plenty of our best carers came from retail, hospitality, or from caring for
their own family. If you are reliable, patient and genuinely interested in
people, we will train you in the rest.

## What you get

- **£12.71 an hour plus 12.07% holiday pay** — £14.24 per hour worked
- **Travel time between calls is paid**
- A full induction and the **Care Certificate** if you are new to care
- Mandatory training kept up to date — safeguarding, moving and handling,
  medication, infection control, fire and first aid
- Online training you can do in your own time through our training platform
- Regular supervision, spot checks and an annual appraisal
- **Someone reachable on call out of hours**, so you are never on your own with
  a difficult situation
- Support towards **NVQ Level 2 and Level 3** if you want to go further
- Shifts that fit your life — tell us when you can work

## What we ask

- That you are reliable and turn up when you said you would
- That you treat people with dignity, on their worst day as well as their best
- All roles are subject to an **enhanced DBS check** and satisfactory references

## Registered nurses

We also supply **registered nurses** to care homes and healthcare settings. If
you are a nurse rather than a carer, apply through the same route and tell us
your registration details when we call.

## Pay

**£12.71 an hour, plus 12.07% holiday pay — £14.24 for every hour you work.**

We would rather show you how that works than quote you one big number:

- **£12.71** is your basic hourly rate. That is the National Living Wage.
- **12.07%** on top is your holiday pay. Because your hours vary week to week,
  we pay it with every wage instead of making you wait until you book time off.
  It appears as its own line on your payslip, so you can always see it.
- Together that is **£14.24 for each hour you actually work**.

**Travel time between calls is paid.** Not every provider does this, and it
makes a real difference to what you take home over a week.

You are also paid mileage, and enrolled in the workplace pension. If a role
carries a different rate — some nursing and specialist shifts do — we will tell
you the figure before you decide whether to go ahead.
