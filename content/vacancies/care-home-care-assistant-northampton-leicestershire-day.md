We are recruiting **care assistants for day shifts in care homes** across
**Northampton and Leicestershire**. If you want to build your hours up, we can
add **daytime home-care visits** around your care-home shifts, so you can put
together the hours that work for you.

## What a day shift is actually like

Days in a care home are busy and sociable. You will help residents get up,
washed and dressed, support them through their meals, help with medication where
you are trained to, and take part in the activities and conversation that make a
home somewhere people actually want to live. Families come in during the day, so
you are often the person they talk to about how their relative is getting on.
Day shifts are the best time to learn the job — there are more staff on, and
more chances to be shown how things are done.

## Where you will work

This is a wider, more spread-out area than our Rugby patch, so where you are
based matters. In and around **Northampton** — and out towards Daventry and
Towcester — there is steady care-home work. Across **Leicestershire**, the
work runs from the city itself out to the market towns like Lutterworth, Market
Harborough and Hinckley. Tell us where you live and we will match you to homes
you can realistically get to, rather than sending you across the county.

## You do not need care experience

Plenty of our best carers came from retail, hospitality, or from caring for
their own family. We train people who are new to care — a full induction, the
Care Certificate, and all the mandatory training the role needs.

## What you get

- **National Living Wage plus 12.07% rolled-up holiday pay**, paid every
  payday — the exact rate is at the top of this page
- **Mileage paid** on any home-care visits you pick up to top up your hours
- A named coordinator who knows you, and **someone on call out of hours**
- Support towards **NVQ Level 2 and 3** if you want to go further
- The full list of what we offer is on our [careers page](/careers)

## What we ask

- That you are reliable and turn up when you said you would
- That you treat people with dignity, on their worst day as well as their best
- All roles are subject to an **enhanced DBS check** and satisfactory references
