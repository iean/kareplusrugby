We are recruiting **registered nurses** for shifts in care homes across
**Rugby and Coventry**. We welcome both **adult nurses (RGN)** and **registered
mental health nurses (RMN)**, and we match you to placements that fit your
registration and the needs of the homes we support.

## Who this is for

- **Registered General Nurses (RGN)** and **Registered Mental Nurses (RMN)**
- You hold a **current NMC registration** with a valid PIN
- You want flexible shifts — days, nights or bank work — rather than a fixed
  rota, and the ability to say when you can and cannot work

## How placements work

We supply nurses to care homes and healthcare settings, and we assign you
according to what each client actually needs. Tell us your registration, your
experience and the kind of setting you work best in, and we place you where that
fits — rather than sending you somewhere that does not.

## Pay

**The rate is agreed at interview**, and it depends on the placement. Unlike our
care-assistant work, which is on a fixed hourly rate, nursing rates vary from one
home or client to another — so we cannot quote a single figure that would hold
for every shift. What we can promise is that we confirm the exact rate with you
before you accept anything: you will never be asked to commit to a shift without
knowing what it pays.

## What you get

- Flexible shifts that fit around your life, booked around your availability
- A named coordinator who knows you, and **someone on call whenever you work**
- **Mileage paid** on any community or home-based work you take on
- Work that suits your registration, rather than a one-size-fits-all placement

## What we ask

- A **current NMC registration** with a valid PIN, checked before every placement
- The **right to work in the UK**
- All roles are subject to an **enhanced DBS check** and satisfactory references
- That you treat people with dignity, on their worst day as well as their best
